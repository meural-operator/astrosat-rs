# AstroSat CZTI Pipeline v3.0.1 vs. Pure-Rust (`astrosat-czti` / `astrosat-cli`) Traceability & Mathematical Audit

**Auditor:** `audit-czti`  
**Legacy Baseline:** `data/ref_src/czti/czti_pipeline/czti/{src,scripts,templates,config}/` (ISRO CZTI Pipeline v3.0.1)  
**Rust Implementation:** `crates/astrosat-czti/src/` (plus `crates/astrosat-core/src/tct.rs` and `crates/astrosat-cli/src/czti_cmd.rs`)  

---

## Executive Summary & Verdict

An exhaustive, line-by-line traceability, bit-level, and mathematical verification audit was performed comparing the legacy ISRO C/C++/Fortran/Python **CZTI Pipeline v3.0.1** against our pure-Rust implementation in `astrosat-czti`, `astrosat-core`, and `astrosat-cli`.

- **Initial Audit Findings Reported (`#55`, `#62`)**: 7 groups of `BUG/MATH_DRIFT` and `PARTIAL_GAP` discrepancies (`CZTI-1`–`CZTI-7`) were identified across `noiseclean.rs` (Generalized v3.0 5-module parity), `evtencor.rs` (`WichmannHillRng` modulus `30323` vs `30319`), `bindata.rs` (`ADDITIONAL_LLD` floor, `cztrspgen` 5x5 `(tx, ty)` open fraction, `COMMON` multi-quadrant combination), `image.rs` (`fft_cross_correlation` `Q2/Q3` `yshift = 1.0` phase ramp & magnitude output, `COMMON` combination), `fits_io.rs` (`CTYPE1B`/`CTYPE2B` `TX---TAN`/`TY---TAN`, aux HDU preservation), `dpigen.rs` (inclusive `b0..=b1` energy bin range), `pipeline.rs` (`entry_stage == 2|3`, missing `cztaddspec`, `.dblevt` output), and `czti_cmd.rs` (hardcoded `already_gcor = true` in `run_czti_bindata`, missing `addspec` / `SSM Data` preservation / `heavybunchclean_quad_with_exposure`). All were fixed and re-verified (`#60`, `#63`, `#64`).
- **Post-Audit Oracle Wave (`#87`, `#88`, `#91`, `#97`)**: Independent legacy oracle cross-checks by `@czti-oracle` caught 3 further parity issues fixed by `@czti-dev` / `@cli-dev`: `image.rs` FFT swap sign / signed frequency axes / `yshift` phase sign, `image.rs` BALCC `SHADOWLIB` layout transpose + `0.10 deg/pix` WCS scale, `bindata.rs` OGIP `.pha` `QUALITY`/`GROUPING` population (`compute_spectrum_quality_and_grouping`), and `czti bindata --ra/--dec` `Option<f64>` fallback to `RA_PNT`/`DEC_PNT`.
- **Neon Self-Review Pass (`#95` -> `#101`, `#103` -> `#117`)**: Deep mathematical re-verification uncovered 4 additional `BUG/MATH_DRIFT`/`PARTIAL_GAP` defects (`CZTI-8`–`CZTI-11`): (1) `flickpixclean_quad` computed per-bin *spatial* sigma instead of per-pixel *temporal* sigma across valid `100 s` bins and clamped the `>=120 ks` `8.0σ` branch down to `6.0σ`; (2) `superbunchclean_quad` pre-filtered single `mag == 1` pixels instead of applying the `3x3` `counter4 > 2` guard pairwise for `ma == 1 && mb == 1 && d > 1.5`; (3) `bindata_quad` `(time_binsize % frac_binsize).abs() < 1e-4` missed remainder wrap-around for non-dyadic `frac_binsize` at AstroSat MET `~2e8 s`; and (4) `pipeline.rs` wrote uncalibrated/uncleaned double events when `write_double_evt == true`. All 4 (plus `@audit-integrity` `#103` response-matrix size & FFT oversampling guards and `@cli-dev` `#116` `run_czti_clean` GTI sync) were fixed and re-verified.
- **Final Verdict**: **PASS (Mathematical & Algorithmic Parity Verified)**. Every CZTI Stage 1, Stage 2 (both `Default` and `Generalized` v3.0 pipelines), Stage 3, `cztaddspec`, TCT interpolation, bit-level decoding, coordinate transformation, ray-tracing, mask-weight renormalization, spectral/response/OGIP `QUALITY`/`GROUPING` generation, and BALCC/FFT imaging matches CZTI Pipeline v3.0.1 (with 6 documented `INTENTIONAL_IMPROVEMENT` fixes over legacy C/C++ bugs and 4 minor non-mathematical `PARTIAL_GAP` items documented below).

## 2. Exact Bit-Level & Mathematical Verification

### 2.1 Telemetry Decoding & Pixel Geometry (`science2event.rs`, `types.rs`)
- **Packet Framing (`l1evtdecode.cpp` vs `science2event.rs:18-24, 181-280`)**:
  - Sync words `SYNC1 = 0xF9A4`, `SYNC2 = 0x2BB1`, 2048-byte packets (`1024` `u16` words).
  - Header word `w[2]` unpacking (`science2event.rs:203-206`): `mml = (w[2] >> 13) & 0x3`, `data_id = (w[2] >> 8) & 0x1F`, `pkt_no = (w[2] >> 4) & 0xF`, `mode_id = w[2] & 0xF`.
  - Packet 0 frame header (`science2event.rs:210-231`): `command = (w[9] << 16) | w[8]`, `second_count = (w[10] << 16) | w[11]`, `boot_page_no = (w[15] >> 14) & 0x3`, `dcnt = w[15] & 0x3FFF`, `wcnt = w[3] - 12`, `quad_id = data_id % 4` (`data_id < 12`). **VERIFIED: `EXACT_MATCH`**.
- **Normal 3-Word & Reduced 2-Word Events (`l1evtdecode.cpp:607-645` vs `science2event.rs:475-498, 698-722`)**:
  - **3-word (`eventsize == 3`, modes `{0,1,4,5}`)**: `cztntick = h[0]`, `cztseccnt = cztntick / 50000.0 + frame_sec`, `pha = ((h[1] >> 4) & 0xFFF) >> 2`, `detid = h[1] & 0xF`, `pixid = (h[2] >> 8) & 0xFF`, `veto = (h[2] >> 1) & 0x7F`, `alpha = h[2] & 0x1`. **VERIFIED: `EXACT_MATCH`**.
  - **2-word (`eventsize == 2`, modes `{2,3,6,7}`)**: `cztntick = (h[0] & 0xFF80) >> 7`, `pha = (((h[0] & 0x7F) << 2) | ((h[1] >> 14) & 0x3)) << 1`, `detid = h[1] & 0xF`, `pixid = (h[1] >> 6) & 0xFF`, `veto = (h[1] >> 5) & 0x1`, `alpha = (h[1] >> 4) & 0x1`. **VERIFIED: `EXACT_MATCH`**.
- **Onboard Bunch Reports (`l1evtdecode.cpp:609, 680-760` vs `science2event.rs:499-697`)**:
  - Triggered on `((h[0] >> 13) & 0x7) == 7 && boot_page_no == 2`, spanning 3 consecutive 3-word records (`dfs = h0[0] & 0x1`, `dsl = (h0[0] >> 1) & 0x3F`, `num_evts = (h0[0] >> 7) & 0x3F`; timestamps `t1 = t2 - dfs/50000`, `t2 = h1[0]/50000 + frame_sec`, `t3 = t2 + dsl/50000`; `detid1..4` nibbles from `h2[0]`). Atomically buffered in `PendingOnboardBunch` so frame truncations cannot desynchronize columns. **VERIFIED: `EXACT_MATCH` (`INTENTIONAL_IMPROVEMENT`)**.
- **Quadrant Coordinate Transforms (`types.rs:97-145`)**:
  - `dx = det_id % 4`, `dy = det_id / 4`, `px = pix_id % 16`, `py = pix_id / 16`, `(detx, dety) = (16*dx + px, 16*dy + py)`.
  - Quadrant reflections: Q0/Q3 flip `dety = 63 - dety`; Q1/Q2 flip `detx = 63 - detx`.
  - Full-plane `detxy_to_locxy`: Q0 `(x, 64+y)`, Q1 `(64+x, 64+y)`, Q2 `(64+x, y)`, Q3 `(x, y)`. Bijectivity formally machine-checked across all 16,384 pixels in `AstrosatFormal/Telemetry.lean` and `czti_tests.rs:35-54`. **VERIFIED: `EXACT_MATCH`**.
